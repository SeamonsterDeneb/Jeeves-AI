Settings

I

Credentials & Access

Obtain an official API key at aistudio.google.com/apikey.

Note for Cloud Speech: Ensure the Cloud Text-to-Speech API is enabled on the same Google Cloud project, and that any key restrictions allow it. This does require a billing account, but Jeeves' voice will switch over to the standard (usually tinny) local voice on your device before you hit that limit, if you ever get to that point, so you'll never be charged for Jeeves to talk to you.

II

Model Orchestration

Populated directly from Google's model catalogue. Google's listing doesn't reveal which models are actually usable by your key — click "Verify" to send a minimal test request and confirm.

Specialized Mode Overrides (Optional)

Leave any mode on "Use default model" to fall back to the primary model above.

III

Persona & Auditory Nuance

How Jeeves ought to address you — e.g. "Sir", "Madam", "Master Wooster".

Adjust playback speed (0.5x to 2.0x). Tap "Test Voice" to unlock audio playback and confirm speech settings on your device.

PWA & Mobile Tip: If no sound plays, ensure your device's physical Silent/Ringer switch is set to ring and media volume is turned up.

IV

Ledger & Cost Accounting

Pricing shifts often and varies by model. Enter your rate from Google's live pricing page.
